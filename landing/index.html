FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work on any anime site?+

It works on most streaming sites by reading the page's title, episode and cover from the URL and metadata. Every site is built differently, so detection is a smart guess — and the save card always lets you fix the title. Anything it misses, you can add manually in one tap.

Where is my data stored? Is it private?+

100% on your own device, using your browser's local storage. There's no account, no server, and nothing is ever uploaded or tracked. Only you can see your list.

What happens when a site gets blocked?+

Nothing — that's the whole point. Your watchlist lives in the extension, not the site. When a mirror dies or gets blocked, open the popup and every anime, status and episode count is still there.

Will it interrupt me while I'm watching?+

Only once per new anime. After you've added something to Watching, new episodes update silently with a tiny toast — no pop-ups blocking your screen mid-binge.

Does it show up on Google or normal sites?+

No. It ignores search engines and general sites, and only activates on real anime watch or detail pages — so searching "anime" on Google won't trigger anything.

Is it really free?+

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no premium tier. Just a tool made for anime fans.
